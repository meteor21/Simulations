How to read this pilot

Three distinctions keep the results interpretable.

01 / CANDIDATES

Registrations need confirmation

The roster contains sourced FEC registration identities. Ballot status, race membership and documented periods in office are separate research facts.

02 / SENTIMENT

Missing is not neutral

Sentiment needs reviewed, candidate-specific evidence and sufficient coverage. A search match alone produces no sentiment score. News portrayal is separate from survey favorability.

03 / TIME & SOURCES

A fixed window, partial coverage

This is a retrospective view of the original collection window. It is not a point-in-time forecast. Source partisanship carries no automatic penalty; no source rating is inferred here.
